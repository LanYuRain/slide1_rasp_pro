01 — The Problem

Problems We Identified

Ghost Bookings & Resource Waste

Frequent no-shows and unattended belongings cause low physical utilization.

Heavy Manual Patrol Overhead

Door-to-door inspections drain staff labor and fail to provide 24/7 oversight.

Privacy Concerns & Legal Constraints

Standard CCTV is strictly restricted in enclosed pods due to privacy laws.

Core Dilemma: Conventional management fails to balance real-time occupancy monitoring with privacy protection.

Enclosed Study Pod: High Privacy Sensitivity
& High Occupancy Demand

02 — Proposed Solution:

Privacy-Preserving Occupancy Monitoring

Edge AI Computing

Real-time on-device video analysis; raw footage never leaves for the cloud, preventing data leaks.

De-identification

Captures only heat signatures, never faces; AI identifies human heat sources to count people.

Remote Oversight

One live dashboard replaces door-to-door checks; rooms left empty too long trigger alerts automatically.
